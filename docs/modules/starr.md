# STARR decision models

*Integration thrust*

## S1. STARR decision models (consumer of the chain)

Household, insurer and government decision models on an annual time step (nested Stackelberg game) driven by the loss timelines.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | Loss timelines (T2); building inventory dynamics; economic conditions |
| **Outputs** | Recommended government policies, expected stakeholder responses, system-wide outcomes |
