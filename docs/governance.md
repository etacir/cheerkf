# Governance

## Project

The CHEER Hub is funded by the U.S. National Science Foundation under the Coastlines and People (CoPe) program and
led by the Disaster Research Center, University of Delaware (PI: Rachel Davidson). Project site:
[drc.udel.edu/cheer](https://www.drc.udel.edu/cheer/).
