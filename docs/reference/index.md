
# Reference pages

Technical pages written by the thrusts for the 2024-26 Knowledge Framework site, kept in full. Authors and dates are
on each page; pages by team members who have since left are marked *emeritus*. Links to repositories that are not
visible to outside visitors are marked *(members only)*.

- [Hazards Thrust](hazards-thrust.md): hazard scenario datasets v1.0 to v1.2 and their file format
- [Integration Thrust](integration-thrust.md): STARR V0, DBI and GOV v1
- [Shovels permit data](shovels-permit-data.md): nationwide building-permit records, cleaned, on DesignSafe
- [FORTUNA](fortuna.md): building inventory generator (superseded)
- [Inventory data](inventory-data.md): Individual.v1 inventory files
- [Deep models](deep-models.md): roof-shape and building-type classifiers
- [Inventory-Loss Link](inventory-loss-link.md): V0 and V1
- [NSI joint inventory processing](nsi-inventory-processing.md): v1.1 pipeline
